# MOM ad compliance checklist (public materials)

Complete **before** boosting, printing, or publishing.  
Official guidance: [mom.gov.sg/employment-agencies](https://www.mom.gov.sg/employment-agencies)

**Asset**: ________________ **Channel**: ☐ FB ☐ IG ☐ Google ☐ GBP ☐ Site ☐ Flyer ☐ Portal  
**Reviewer**: ________________ **Date**: ______________

## Must have
- ☐ EA **full name** and **licence number** on the advertisement / publicity material  
- ☐ Claims are true and verifiable (training types, process, stats)  
- ☐ Helper biodata/photos (if public): written consent logged + dignified presentation  

## Must NOT appear in public ads
- ☐ Agency fees, discounts, promotions, “$0”, cheap package prices  
- ☐ Helper salaries, loans, payment modes (e.g. post-dated cheques) as ad bait  
- ☐ Merchandise language: “free replacement”, “fast delivery”, “stock”, “grab now”  
- ☐ Trait marketing: “obedient”, “compliant”, “smart”, “hardworking”  
- ☐ MOM logo or implied MOM endorsement  
- ☐ Undignified display of helpers (promo attire, “on display” framing)  

## Allowed angles (examples)
- ☐ Care-type education (“What we check for elderly-care matching”)  
- ☐ Process clarity (consult → match → IPA → settle)  
- ☐ Verifiable training offered  
- ☐ Placement / retention statistics **if accurate**  
- ☐ CTA: “Tell us your care needs” / “Book a consultation”
