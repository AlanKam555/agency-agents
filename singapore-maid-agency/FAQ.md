# FAQ — adopting the Singapore Maid Agency Kit

## Is this only for one agency?
No. It is **MIT-licensed** for any licensed EA in Singapore. Put **your** EA full name and licence number on ads and documents.

## Do we need Cursor or coding skills?
No. Most agencies should start with:
1. [START-HERE.md](START-HERE.md) Path A  
2. [CHAT-PROMPTS.md](CHAT-PROMPTS.md) in ChatGPT / Claude / Gemini  
3. [toolkit/](toolkit/) forms in Google Drive  

## Is this legal advice?
No. Agents produce checklists and drafts. Confirm fees, insurance, advertising, and Work Permit steps on [mom.gov.sg](https://www.mom.gov.sg/).

## Can we advertise our agency fee publicly?
Generally **no** for MDW-placing EA public ads under MOM advertising guidance — fee bait and merchandise language are high risk. Share fee schedules **on request / in consultation**. Use [toolkit/ad-compliance-checklist.md](toolkit/ad-compliance-checklist.md) before every boost.

## Will AI replace my consultants?
No. It standardises intake, matching scorecards, compliance checklists, and ad review so consultants spend time on judgment and relationships.

## What should we roll out first (Week 1)?
1. Ad compliance checklist on all live posts  
2. Lead qualification fields before biodata  
3. Needs Brief + max-3 shortlist rule  
See [START-HERE.md](START-HERE.md) first-week plan.

## How do Mandarin-speaking staff use this?
Use [STAFF-CHEATSHEET.md](STAFF-CHEATSHEET.md) 中文 activations, [toolkit/whatsapp-macros.md](toolkit/whatsapp-macros.md), and the 中文 section in [CHAT-PROMPTS.md](CHAT-PROMPTS.md).

## How do we share with other branches?
```bash
./scripts/install-singapore-maid-agency.sh --tool folder
```
Zip the `singapore-maid-agency-kit` folder and put it in Drive / USB.

## What if a match fails in month 2?
Use Replacement & Refund Specialist (or the chat prompt). Screen Rule 13A refund eligibility; do not invent unlawful “no refund” terms.

## How do we know matching is improving?
Fill [toolkit/weekly-kpi.md](toolkit/weekly-kpi.md) every Monday. Track Day-90/180 survival and refunds by care type and lead source.
