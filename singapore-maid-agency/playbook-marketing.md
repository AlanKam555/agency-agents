# Playbook: Employer Acquisition Marketing (Singapore Maid Agency)

> Use with: Employer Acquisition Marketer → MOM Ad Compliance Reviewer → Google Ads Lead Specialist → Local SEO & Reputation → Employer Content Strategist → Sales Closer

## Goal
Generate **qualified employer consultations** that become durable placements — without MOM advertising violations.

## Non-negotiables (MOM)
Public ads/pages must **not** include:
- Fees, discounts, salaries, loans, payment modes  
- Merchandise language (“free replacement”, “fast delivery”)  
- Trait marketing (“obedient”, “hardworking”)  

Public ads/pages **must**:
- Show EA full name + licence number  
- Keep helper dignity; biodata public only with consent  

Fees: share on request / in consult — not as bait.

## 30-day launch sequence

### Week 1 — Foundation
1. Content Strategist: 5 FAQ pillars (process, infant, elderly, transfer, first 30 days)  
2. Local SEO: GBP fix + NAP = MOM directory  
3. Compliance Reviewer: glossary of banned phrases for the team  

### Week 2 — Capture intent
1. Google Ads: Brand + Generic hire + Transfer (compliant RSAs)  
2. Landers + care-type forms → Sales SLA 15 min  
3. Compliance Reviewer: Pass before go-live  

### Week 3 — Trust & education
1. Meta: care-type education creatives (boost only after compliance Pass)  
2. Review request flow post Day-30  
3. WhatsApp auto-reply with care-type questions  

### Week 4 — Optimise quality
1. Acquisition Marketer + Retention Analyst: kill channels with poor Day-90 survival  
2. Negatives for job-seeker queries  
3. Double down on care-type campaigns that Sales converts  

## Funnel KPI tree
```
Impressions → Clicks → Employer leads → Qualified consults → Signed → Day-180 alive
Optimize to: Cost / Qualified consult AND Day-180 survival by source
```

## Creative testing (safe)
| Test | Compliant angle |
|------|-----------------|
| A/B hooks | Process clarity vs care-type specialism |
| Proof | Training types, retention %, licensed status |
| CTA | “Tell us your care needs” vs “Book consult” |
| Never test | Fee amounts, free replacement, trait adjectives |

## Handoff to matching
Every paid/organic lead should arrive with:
- Care type  
- Timeline  
- First-time employer Y/N  
- Language preference  

→ Sales Closer qualifies → Employer Advisor (if first-time) → Matching playbook  

## Anti-patterns
- Boosting “$888 package” posts  
- Portal-only dependency with no brand/GBP  
- WhatsApp-only ads with zero qualification  
- Measuring success by raw lead count alone
