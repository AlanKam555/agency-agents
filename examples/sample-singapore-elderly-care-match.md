# Worked example: Elderly-care match (Singapore EA)

> Filled sample so consultants can copy the pattern. Names are fictional.

## 1) Lead (Sales)

**Channel:** Google Ads — “elderly caregiver helper Singapore”  
**Employer:** Mrs Tan, Tampines, first-time employer, Mandarin + English  
**Ask:** Helper for mother (78), post-stroke, needs shower assist + companionship, within 3 weeks  

**Qualification captured:**
- Care type: Elderly  
- Timeline: 2–3 weeks  
- First-time: Yes (EOP not done)  
- Pathway: Transfer preferred for speed, open to ex-SG  

**Sales reply:** Used lead macro → booked 15-min consult → handed to Employer Advisor for cost sheet (private).

## 2) Needs Brief (Matching)

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Playbook | Elderly |
| Household | 4-room HDB, 2 adults + elderly mother, no pets |
| Primary job | Shower assist, mobility support, meds reminder, companionship |
| Secondary | Light housework, simple Chinese meals |
| Out of scope | Overnight hospital stay, heavy lifting alone |
| Rest day | Sunday; off-in-lieu if family outing |
| Language | Mandarin strong; basic English OK |
| Risk flags | First-time employer; mobility care |

## 3) Shortlist (max 3)

| Ref | Score | Evidence | Decision |
|-----|-------|----------|----------|
| MY-118 | 4.4 | 2 yrs wheelchair shower assist; Mandarin; Sun OK | Strong |
| PH-092 | 3.8 | Eldercare cert; English stronger than Mandarin | Conditional — language interview |
| ID-055 | 2.9 | General housework only; “willing to learn” eldercare | Do not recommend |

**Cover note sent:** 2 fits + 1 conditional; asked Mrs Tan to confirm shower assist frequency and whether night toileting is required.

## 4) Interview (Assessor)

PH-092 Mandarin comprehension: Conditional fail for this household → dropped.  
MY-118 safety + transfer demo answers: **Pass**.

## 5) Compliance → Placement

- EOP completed before IPA  
- Bond + MI/PA submitted pre-arrival  
- Safety agreement signed on handover  
- Day-3 / Day-7 check-ins scheduled  

## 6) Why this is “good matching”

- Care-type hard gate blocked housework-only biodata  
- Shortlist stayed at 3  
- Language risk tested before IPA  
- First-time employer got cost transparency privately (not via fee ad)
